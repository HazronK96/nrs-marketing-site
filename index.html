Features

Everything a gamer needs to stay in the loop

NRS is designed around one idea: get the real story, fast, without the clickbait.

🎮

Game-first categories

Jump straight to Nintendo, PlayStation, Xbox, PC or Reviews — or browse everything in one unified feed.

⭐

Key-point summaries

Every article leads with a bullet-point summary, so you grasp the story in seconds before diving deeper.

🔗

Verified sources

Each release links back to its original outlets — read with confidence and follow the trail yourself.

🔖

Bookmarks & profiles

Save stories for later and build a profile around the topics and platforms you care about most.

✍️

Create & publish

Authors can draft and publish releases through a dedicated creation flow, with tasks to manage the pipeline.

📈

Trending & topics

Discover what the community is reading with Trending News and recommended topics like Esports and Indie Games.

How it works

From headline to the full story in three steps

1

Discover

Open a clean, sortable feed and filter by your platform. Trending and recommended topics surface what matters now.

2

Read smart

Lead with the key points, then read the full release — every claim backed by linked original sources.

3

Save & share

Bookmark stories, manage them from your profile, and — as an author — create and publish your own releases.

Who it's for

Made for gamers who want signal, not noise

Gaming news moves fast and spreads thin across dozens of sites. NRS pulls it together, trims it down, and keeps it honest — so you spend less time hunting and more time playing.

  • Console & PC players tracking releases, prices and updates
  • Fans who want a quick, trustworthy summary at a glance
  • Readers who care where a story actually came from
  • Writers who want a simple way to publish game news
